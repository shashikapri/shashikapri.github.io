Chapter 1.2

Who Should Read This Guidebook?

US-2 Audience Overview

The right readers for this guide

This guidebook is written for people who plan, coordinate, and deliver projects in real-world settings. You do not need to be an expert to start. It is designed for both beginners building a foundation and experienced practitioners who want a practical reference.

The content combines structure, actionable methods, and examples so different project roles can quickly apply what they need.

Primary audience (US-2)

Scrum Masters

Use this guide to facilitate teams, remove delivery blockers, and strengthen Agile routines such as planning, reviews, and retrospectives.

Project Managers

Apply the methods to scope work, plan schedules, manage risks, and communicate clear progress to stakeholders.

Project Team Members

Understand key concepts, shared terminology, and delivery practices so team collaboration stays aligned and efficient.

Also useful for

  • Students learning project management and Agile practices.
  • Newly assigned team leads transitioning into PM responsibilities.
  • Product owners and business analysts working closely with delivery teams.
  • Stakeholders who want to understand project workflows and decision points.

How each reader can use it

  1. Start with your immediate challenge or chapter topic.
  2. Scan the key concept boxes and examples first.
  3. Apply the checklist or framework in your current project.
  4. Return as a reference during planning, execution, and review.

Key takeaway: if you are responsible for delivering project outcomes, supporting a delivery team, or learning project leadership, this guidebook is meant for you.
