Chapter 11.2

AI Usage Transparency

US-36 Ethics & Disclosure

This page explains how course materials, outside references, and AI support were used while creating the guidebook.

User Story (US-36)

As a course participant, I want AI usage to be transparently reported, so that the assignment follows course rules and ethical guidelines.

What Was Used

The guidebook content was developed using course materials as the main foundation, together with additional references from reputable project management and agile sources. YouTube videos were also used as supporting material to make some topics easier to understand for users.

These sources were used to improve clarity, verify concepts, and keep the assignment aligned with the course requirements. Where appropriate, the final chapters already include source citations so readers can trace the material back to the original references.

How AI Was Used

Formatting and structure

AI support was used for parts of the formatting, structuring, and initial GitHub Pages layout so the pages stayed consistent and readable.

Content support

AI was used as a drafting aid for organizing the presentation of the content, but the final wording was checked against the intended course topic and references.

What AI did not create

The core instructional idea and the assignment direction were not created by AI. They came from the course task, the guidebook topic, and the user’s requirements.

Human responsibility

The final content selection, ethical disclosure, and source usage remain the responsibility of the author and course participant.

Disclosure Statement

This guidebook was prepared by combining course materials, additional references, and selected YouTube resources for clarification. AI assistance was used for formatting, structuring, and the initial GitHub Pages layout. The underlying instructional idea and assignment direction were not generated by AI.

Ethics Checklist

  • All outside sources should be cited in the relevant chapter reference sections.
  • YouTube videos should be used as support and not presented as original authored content.
  • AI support should be disclosed clearly when it is used for layout, structure, or drafting help.
  • The final submission should remain the responsibility of the human author.
Summary: This chapter documents the use of course materials, other references, and YouTube support, while also disclosing that AI helped with formatting and initial layout only.
