Creating and Submitting a Change Request

Change Request Form 4 Essential Elements

What to Include in Every Change Request

When you create a change request (using a tool like Jira, ServiceNow, or a standardized form), you must include the following four elements:

1. What needs to change? (Clear description of the modification)
2. Why does it need to change? (Business or technical justification)
3. What is the expected outcome? (Success criteria)
4. What is your preliminary risk assessment? (Low, Medium, High – with a brief explanation)

Note: In formal change management systems, these fields are captured on standardized forms to ensure consistency across all requests.

Creating and Submitting a Change Request

All changes must enter the process through a formal Change Request. This ensures that every proposal is documented, traceable, and contains enough information for initial evaluation.

Where to Submit

Submit your completed request to the designated Change Authority (e.g., your Project Manager, Scrum Master, or the Change Advisory Board portal). In Agile teams, this may mean adding it as a user story to the product backlog for prioritization.
