Chapter 1.4

Using this Guidebook for Self-Assessment?

US-3 Self-Assessment Guide

Check your readiness and alignment

Before you dive deeper into this guidebook, take a moment to assess whether your current project needs match the topics covered here. This self-assessment helps you identify which sections will be most valuable for your situation and ensures you focus on what matters most to your team.

Use this as a quick checkpoint to align your expectations with the guidebook's scope and depth.

Is this guidebook right for your project? (US-3)

Yes, if your project has:

  • A defined scope with deliverables and timelines.
  • A team that needs clear coordination and accountability.
  • Regular planning, execution, and review phases.
  • A need for both structured planning and adaptive responses.
  • Stakeholders who expect progress visibility and quality outcomes.

Limited fit, if your project is:

  • Highly unstructured with no defined milestones or deliverables.
  • Purely experimental with no success criteria or stakeholder reporting.
  • A one-person task with no team coordination needed.
  • Operating in a completely novel domain with zero reference materials.
  • Focused only on research with no delivery expectations.
