1. Scrum Roles: The Team Structure

Scrum doesn't use traditional "Manager" titles. Instead, it uses three specific roles to ensure balance and progress.

Product Owner (The "Visionary")

They decide what needs to be built. They manage the Product Backlog and ensure the team delivers value to the stakeholders.

Scrum Master (The "Coach")

They make sure the team follows Scrum principles. They remove "impediments" (roadblocks) and facilitate meetings. They do not tell people what to do; they help the team work better.

Development Team (The "Creators")

A cross-functional group (designers, writers, coders) who do the actual work. They are self-organizing and decide how to do the work.

2. Scrum Events: The Heartbeat of the Project

Scrum uses "timeboxed" events to create a predictable rhythm and allow for regular inspection.

Event Frequency Purpose
The Sprint Weekly (for this project) The container for all other events. A fixed time period where a "Done" increment is created.
Sprint Planning Every other Friday The team meets to decide which User Stories from the Backlog will be moved to the Sprint.
Daily Scrum Optional (per assignment) A 15-minute meeting to sync activities and create a plan for the next 24 hours.
Sprint Review Every other Friday The team presents the "Done" work to the Product Owner for feedback. (Your Jan 23rd presentation is a Sprint Review!)
Sprint Retrospective Every other Friday Occurs after the Review. The team discusses what went well and what could be improved for the next sprint.
