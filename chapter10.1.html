Project Management Associations

US-32 Professional Communities

Learn where project managers and Scrum professionals connect, learn, and grow.

User Story

As a project manager/Scrum master/project team member, I want a brief overview of project management associations, so that I know where to find professional communities and resources.

What Are Project Management Associations?

Project management associations are professional organizations that provide certifications, training, networking opportunities, and best practices. They help professionals stay updated, improve skills, and connect with global communities.

Meet the Main Associations

PMI (Project Management Institute)

Best for: Traditional & hybrid project managers

  • Globally recognized (PMP, CAPM)
  • Strong standards & frameworks
  • Great for career advancement

PRINCE2 (PeopleCert / AXELOS)

Best for: Structured, process-driven projects

  • Very popular in Europe
  • Clear roles and processes
  • Widely used in government projects

Scrum Alliance

Best for: Beginners in Agile & Scrum

  • Certified ScrumMaster (CSM)
  • Strong community focus
  • Interactive, practical training

Scrum.org

Best for: Deep Scrum knowledge

  • Founded by Ken Schwaber
  • More rigorous certification exams
  • Strong focus on theory + practice

IPMA

Best for: Competence-based development

  • Focus on real skills, not just theory
  • Levels based on experience
  • Strong European presence

Why Join These Associations?

Networking

Connect with professionals, mentors, and industry experts.

Learning

Access courses, certifications, webinars, and industry knowledge.

Career Growth

Improve job opportunities and professional credibility.

Best Practices

Learn proven frameworks and standards used worldwide.

Summary: Project management associations provide valuable resources, certifications, and communities. Joining them helps professionals grow, stay updated, and connect with the global project management ecosystem.
