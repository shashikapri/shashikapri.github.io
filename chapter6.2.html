Chapter 6.2

Understanding Project Risks

US-23 Risk Types

1. Identifying Risks

It is crucial to identify project risks to ensure that potential problems are recognized early and managed before they affect the project.

There are different types of risks that can occur during a project. Understanding these helps the project manager or Scrum master identify and classify them more effectively.

2. Identifying and Classifying Risks

Risks can be identified by reviewing the project plan, user stories, and tasks, and by discussing possible problems within the team.

Once identified, risks can be classified into categories such as technical, project management, team, external, and quality risks.

Risks are also evaluated based on probability and impact. Probability describes how likely the risk is to occur, while impact shows how serious the consequences would be.

Technical Risks

Related to system or technology issues such as bugs, integration problems, or tools not working as expected.

Project Management Risks

Related to planning and organization, such as unclear requirements, poor scheduling, or lack of resources.

Team Risks

Connected to team members, including lack of experience, poor communication, or unavailability.

External Risks

Come from outside the project, such as requirement changes, stakeholder decisions, or dependencies.

Quality Risks

Occur when the final product does not meet expected standards due to poor testing or misunderstood requirements.

Summary

Identify: Review plans, tasks, and team discussions
Classify: Technical · Management · Team · External · Quality
Evaluate: Probability · Impact
