Chapter 9.3

Agile Certifications: PSM and CSM

US-34 Professional Development

A practical guide for Scrum Masters to compare certification options and build a realistic learning plan.

User Story (US-34)

As a Scrum master, I want information about useful agile project management certifications, so that I can plan my professional development.

Why These Two Certifications?

PSM (Professional Scrum Master) and CSM (Certified ScrumMaster) are two of the most recognized entry points for Scrum Masters.

Industry Recognition

Both are widely recognized by employers across industries.

Role Relevance

Directly aligned with facilitation, coaching, and team improvement.

Career Growth

Can support progression to Agile Coach or Delivery Lead roles.

Learning Structure

Provides a clear syllabus for disciplined skill development.

PSM vs CSM at a Glance

Criteria PSM I CSM
Provider Scrum.org Scrum Alliance
Training Requirement Not mandatory Mandatory course with approved trainer
Assessment Style Online exam, strong focus on Scrum Guide understanding Course + exam, practical classroom context
Difficulty Generally considered more exam-intensive Generally more structured for beginners
Renewal Model No recurring renewal for PSM I Renewal required with fees and learning credits
Best For Self-directed learners confident with exam prep Learners who prefer instructor-led foundations

Costs, exam format, and policy details can change. Always confirm on official provider pages before registering.

Suggested 8-Week Development Plan

Weeks 1-2

Build Scrum Foundation

Read the Scrum Guide, review accountabilities, events, and artifacts, and map concepts to your current team context.

Weeks 3-4

Practice and Reflection

Use scenario questions, observe your ceremonies, and capture improvement opportunities in a learning journal.

Weeks 5-6

Certification Focus

Choose PSM or CSM path, complete targeted preparation, and close weak areas like metrics, facilitation, or coaching.

Weeks 7-8

Exam Readiness

Take timed mock exams, review mistakes, and schedule your exam when your scores are consistently stable.

How to Choose the Right Path

Choose PSM if you want...

  • flexible self-study and direct exam path
  • strong theory validation against the Scrum Guide
  • a certification without periodic renewal requirement

Choose CSM if you want...

  • guided learning with instructor support
  • interactive discussion and practical workshop format
  • a structured first step into Scrum facilitation
Summary: Both PSM and CSM are valuable for Scrum Masters. PSM often suits independent learners who want exam-focused depth, while CSM suits learners who benefit from trainer-led structure. The best choice depends on your learning style, budget, and career goals.
