Beyond Scrum: Understanding Other Agile Approaches

Why This Matters for Scrum Masters

As a Scrum Master, understanding other frameworks expands your toolkit, helps you collaborate in hybrid environments, and prepares you for organizational scaling or transformation initiatives (Rubin, 2012; Project Management Institute, 2021).

1. When to Borrow from XP

Extreme Programming
Situation: Technical debt is accumulating, quality is declining (Beck, 2004)

Adaptable Practices:

  • Introduce pair programming during complex backlog items
  • Add TDD workshops to Sprint Retrospectives
  • Implement continuous integration alongside Definition of Done
Watch out: Don't overwhelm teams; introduce 1-2 practices per Sprint

2. When SAFe Becomes Relevant

Scaled Agile Framework
Situation: Your organization is growing or adopting SAFe (Leffingwell, 2017)

Your Role Evolution:

Scrum Master SAFe Scrum Master (within an Agile Release Train)
  • Additional responsibilities: PI Planning facilitation, ART sync coordination
  • New collaboration: Working with Release Train Engineers (RTEs)

Preparation:

Understand PI Planning, inspect & adapt at program level

3. When FDD Concepts Can Enhance Scrum

Feature-Driven Development
Situation: Complex domains requiring clear modeling (Palmer & Felsing, 2002)

Complementary Practices:

  • Use domain modeling during refinement for complex features
  • Apply feature decomposition techniques to large backlog items
  • Adopt progress tracking methods for transparency in large Sprints

Integration:

Feature-driven thinking without abandoning user stories
